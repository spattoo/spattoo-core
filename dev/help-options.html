Element help — four options

All four run on the same five REAL elements from the dev library, and all four derive everything from the element's own allowed_zones / placement_config / allowed_actions — nothing is authored per element. Each page states plainly what it fakes.

1 · The element demonstrates itselfbuilt first
It flies through every pose it can strike, with a caption naming each. Has a speed control. Known gap: on top / rim / board you often can't see it — needs per-zone camera framing.
2 · Test-drive bubble
A cake of your own to ruin, then transfer the exact instance you built to the real cake. Teaches by doing. Costs a second placement surface that can drift from the real one.
3 · Chips that perform
Tap "Tilt it" and it tilts. Always visible, no gesture to discover, plus a zone map that answers "where does it go?" instantly. Cheapest — and it's a toolbar, which people stop seeing.
4 · Help that finds you
Nothing on screen until you're stuck: two rejected drops and the cake lights up where it CAN go. Invisible to anyone who already knows — and to anyone who gives up after one try.
